Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 50 Yard Cards ("we," "us," or "the Site") collects, uses, and protects information when you use this site, a virtual trading card collection game built around indoor football. By using 50 Yard Cards, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: username, password (stored in hashed form), and email address if you provide one during sign-up.
  • Gameplay data: your card collection, pack-opening history, coin balance, marketplace listings and trades, trivia results, and leaderboard stats.
  • Local device storage: we use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in (an authentication token) and to remember basic account details, and may temporarily store unsynced collection data locally before it's saved to our servers.
  • Technical data: standard request information such as IP address, browser type, and device information, collected automatically by our hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare) for security and performance purposes.

We do not knowingly collect any information beyond what's needed to operate your account and gameplay.

How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your account and card collection
  • To operate core features such as pack opening, trading, the marketplace, trivia, and leaderboards
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of numbered/limited card enforcement rules
  • To communicate with you about your account, such as password resets or important site updates
  • To maintain and improve site performance and reliability

Cookies and Local Storage

50 Yard Cards uses browser local storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to keep you logged in and to cache basic account and collection data for a faster experience. Clearing your browser's site data will sign you out and remove any locally cached information.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information in the following cases:

  • Public gameplay data: your username, packs opened, card collection stats, and leaderboard ranking are visible to other users as part of normal site functionality.
  • Marketplace and trades: when you list or trade cards, your username and listing details are visible to other users involved in or browsing that activity.
  • Service providers: we use Cloudflare (Workers, Pages, and D1 database) to host and run the site. These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by their own security and privacy practices.
  • Legal requirements: if required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of the Site or its users.

Data Retention

We retain account and gameplay data for as long as your account remains active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, contact us using the information below.

Your Choices

  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data at any time.
  • You can sign out at any point, which clears your local authentication token from this device.

Virtual Items

Cards, coins, and other in-site items have no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for real currency. All virtual items exist solely within 50 Yard Cards and may be modified, rebalanced, or removed at our discretion.

Children's Privacy

50 Yard Cards is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be sent to contact@indoorfootballindex.com.
